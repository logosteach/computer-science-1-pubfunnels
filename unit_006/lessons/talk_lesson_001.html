Lesson 001 - Introduction to OOP: Talking Points & Discussion

Key talking points and discussion questions to help guide your teaching of this lesson.

Main Talking Points

  • OOP definition: Organize programs around objects that combine data and behavior.

  • Main benefits: organization, reusability, and modeling real-world or problem-domain things.

  • Core vocabulary: class, object/instance, attribute, method.

  • Students already use objects: strings, lists, and dictionaries are objects with methods.

  • Complexity management: OOP helps larger programs stay understandable by grouping related ideas.

Discussion & Thought Questions

  • What is the difference between a class and an object?

  • Name three real-world things you might model as classes in a program.

  • How does "hello".upper() show that you have already used methods?

  • When might functions alone be enough, and when might classes help more?

  • How does creation “according to their kinds” in Genesis relate to the idea of blueprints and classes?

Biblical Integration

God created the world with distinct kinds of things, each with purpose and design. Classes as blueprints are a small human echo of ordered creation.

Scripture:
“Then God said, ‘Let the earth bring forth grass, the herb that yields seed, and the fruit tree that yields fruit according to its kind...’ And it was so.” (Genesis 1:11)
