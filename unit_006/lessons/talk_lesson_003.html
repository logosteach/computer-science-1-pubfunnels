Lesson 003 - Attributes, __init__, and Encapsulation: Talking Points & Discussion

Key talking points and discussion questions to help guide your teaching of this lesson.

Main Talking Points

  • __init__: Special method that initializes a new instance.

  • self: Refers to the specific object being created or used.

  • Dot notation: Access and update attributes with obj.attr.

  • Encapsulation: Bundle data with behavior and control how data is changed.

  • Safer design: Methods can validate values before updating internal state.

Discussion & Thought Questions

  • Why does almost every instance method include self as the first parameter?

  • What happens if you forget to assign an attribute in __init__ but later try to use it?

  • Why might direct attribute assignment be dangerous for something like a bank balance?

  • How does a leading underscore (for example, _balance) communicate intent in Python?

  • How do unique attributes and wise boundaries in a person relate to encapsulation?
