Lesson 007 - Putting It All Together with Classes: Talking Points & Discussion

Key talking points and discussion questions to help guide your teaching of this lesson.

Main Talking Points

  • Design by nouns: Identify the “things” in the problem — those often become classes.

  • Combine OOP tools: multiple classes, inheritance, polymorphism, encapsulation, abstraction.

  • Blend prior skills: loops, conditionals, lists, and functions still belong inside methods.

  • Debugging OOP: check instantiation, self, attribute initialization, and method targets.

  • Purposeful design: good class structure is orderly and relational, not random.

Discussion & Thought Questions

  • How would you design classes for a school library or sports team roster?

  • Where should validation live — in the caller or in the class methods?

  • What are the first three things you check when an OOP program fails?

  • How can inheritance and polymorphism reduce repeated if/elif type checks?

  • How does careful design reflect the character of God as Creator and Sustainer?

Biblical Integration

Well-designed classes can reflect God’s character as the ultimate Designer — orderly, purposeful, and relational. Our designs are limited, but they can still honor the One who holds all things together.

“For since the creation of the world His invisible attributes are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made...” (Romans 1:20)
