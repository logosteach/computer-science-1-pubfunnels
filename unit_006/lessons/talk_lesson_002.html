Lesson 002 - Creating Classes and Objects: Talking Points & Discussion

Key talking points and discussion questions to help guide your teaching of this lesson.

Main Talking Points

  • class keyword: Defines a new type / blueprint.

  • Creating instances: Call the class name like a function: obj = ClassName().

  • Blueprint vs object: One class; many independent instances.

  • Naming style: CapWords for classes; snake_case for instance variables.

  • type and isinstance: Help students verify what they created.

Discussion & Thought Questions

  • What does pass mean inside a class, and why might we use it temporarily?

  • If a = Dog() and b = Dog(), are a and b the same object?

  • Why is class naming convention important for readability?

  • What real-world system would you model with a class this week?

  • How does God as Creator differ from our limited act of designing classes?

Biblical Integration

God is the ultimate Creator who designs unique things. Writing classes and creating instances is a small derivative creativity under His lordship.
