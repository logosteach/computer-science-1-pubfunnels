Truthy and Falsy

In this lesson, we will explore the concept of truthy and falsy values in Python. This is an important topic that will help you write more concise and efficient code. We will also reflect on how this concept relates to our understanding of truth from a Christ centered perspective.

Objectives

By the end of the lesson, learners will be able to:

  • Explain the concept of truthy and falsy values in Python.

  • Identify which values evaluate to True and which evaluate to False in a Boolean context.

  • Use implicit Boolean checking (e.g., if name: instead of if name != "").

  • Understand the difference between explicit == True and implicit truthy evaluation.

  • Reflect on the fact that God sees things perfectly as they truly are. Nothing is hidden from His sight. From our perspective, truth can seem relative sometimes, and uncertain. We are prone to think that God Himself thinks and see things this way as well. Yet, in Christ Jesus we have direct access to our Heavenly Father. He is teaching us, training us in godliness in Christ Jesus so that the falsehoods of the world will become clearer, and we will be prepared for every good work in Christ Jesus. (Romans 12:2, Titus 2:11-12, 1 John 2:17)

Anchored in Christ

Living Truthy

If you are familiar with the New Testament, even a little, it is easy to see that all believers are commanded and encouraged to live and walk in the truth. We are considered lights in the world. We are not the true light. That is Christ, but we are, if I may use the phrase, truthy lights, if we are living in obedience to Christ and His word. The world sees us, the truthy representations of Christ, and that should lead them to ask: "What is it in them that I see that I don't really like, but I must confess that this is the way that life is truly meant to be lived?"

Do all things without complaining and disputing, that you may become blameless and harmless, children of God without fault in the midst of a crooked and perverse generation, among whom you shine as lights in the world, holding fast the word of life…” - Philippians 2:14-16
