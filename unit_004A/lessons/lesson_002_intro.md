# Narration Script: Comparator Operators

Today we’re moving forward in our unit by exploring **comparator operators** in Python.

These operators allow us to **compare** values — things like greater than, less than, equal to, or not equal to. They return Boolean values: `True` or `False`.

Just as the Scripture asks in **Isaiah 40:18**: “To whom will you compare me? Or who is my equal?” says the Holy One.

In the same way, when we write code, we are constantly making comparisons. Is this value greater than that one? Are these two things equal? These comparisons help our programs make decisions.

Let’s look at the main comparator operators:
- `>`  Greater than
- `<`  Less than
- `>=` Greater than or equal to
- `<=` Less than or equal to
- `==` Equal to
- `!=` Not equal to
