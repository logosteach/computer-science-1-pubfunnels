Talking Points - Comparator Operators

Use these talking points to guide class discussion, video lecture, or student review.

1. The Six Comparator Operators

  • == Equal to
  • != Not equal to
  • > Greater than
  • < Less than
  • >= Greater than or equal to
  • <= Less than or equal to
  • Discussion: Why is == different from = (assignment)?

2. Comparing Numbers, Strings, and Variables

  • Numbers: age >= 18, score >= 70
  • Strings: Case-sensitive comparisons
  • Variables: Store results in Boolean variables
  • Activity: Predict the result of several expressions together.

3. Using Comparators in Decision Making

  • Foundation of if statements
  • Real-world example: VBS age check or grade evaluation
  • Emphasize: Comparators produce Boolean results that control program flow.
