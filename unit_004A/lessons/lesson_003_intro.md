# Narration Script: Compound Conditional Statements

In this lesson, we’re going to be learning about **compound conditional statements** — using the `and`, `or`, `xor`, and `not` keywords in Python.

Scripture is high, the law of God is high, and perhaps the highest of all commandments — one of the most important — is this: 

“Love the Lord your God with all your heart **and** with all your soul **and** with all your mind **and** with all your strength.” (Deuteronomy 6:5)

And the second is like it: “Love your neighbor as yourself.”

In Deuteronomy, God gives a series of commands of love. It’s not simply “love the Lord your God” — He connects it with **and** statements: love the Lord your God with all your heart, **and** with all your soul, **and** with all your mind, **and** with all your strength.

Jesus Christ has done exactly this. And because He has loved us first, we now — through the power of the Spirit and regeneration from darkness into light — can now love God the way that He deserves. Our love is not a perfect love, but by faith in Christ Jesus and by His grace, it is a genuine love.

So as we begin this lesson to learn how to make our conditional statements more complex using these compound operators, let us understand and have trust in the fact that Christ Jesus has done the hard work. Now it’s simply for us to trust Him.
