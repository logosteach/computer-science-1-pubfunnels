# Creating A Computer Lab Rules

When creating a computer lab, please us the templates/LogosTeach_CS1_Lab_Template.pdf file. A lab will sometimes be 15 minutes in length but not always. Please ask me ahead of time, if I do not specify, how long a computer lab should be before writing one.

At then end of each computer lab, beginning on a new page, should be explanations and solutions for the teacher/student to review. I want to keep the lab and the solutions all in one file.

Please make sure to mention common mistakes and pitfalls that new learners encounter.
