# Anchored in Christ

Each lesson ends with a section called "Anchored in Christ". In this section I like to make comparisons, contrasts, and parallels between what the we are learning in the lesson and how this anchors us/or is different from how the believer is anchored in Christ by faith alone.

## Beginning an Anchored in Christ Section

You will know that I wish to begin developing an Anchored in Christ section when I type the command: AIC in the Grok browser terminal.

## What to do

When we arrive at this section, there are a series of steps I would like to follow.

1. Please ask me how I would like to structure this section. Comparison, Contrast, Parallel, Other, Not sure yet?
2. Ask me the idea or topic or theme I would like to weave into this section.
3. Once you have an idea, theme or topic, find several scripture references that might fit this theme or topic as a suggestion.
4. All scripture references should be in the NKJV please.
