Arithmetic, Variable Naming Rules and Conventions

In this lesson we will explore the basics of using the Python shell to perform arithmetic operations and understand important variable assignments, and the naming rules that come along with creating variables in Python. These elements will be foundational for all our future learning and work in this course. We will also reflect on the interactive nature of the Python shell and how this can parallel our relationship with God through Christ Jesus.

Objectives

By the end of the lesson, learners will be able to:

  • Open and use the Python IDLE interactive shell.

  • Execute simple Python commands and expressions directly in the shell.

  • Understand the difference between the interactive shell and running saved scripts.

  • Use basic arithmetic operators and see immediate results in the shell.

  • Recognize and correct simple syntax errors in the interactive environment.

  • Reflect on how the interactive nature of the Python IDLE shell (where we type commands and receive immediate feedback) reminds us that our relationship with God is not passive; rather, we are called to actively engage in worship and adoration, listening to the preached word of God, read the mouth of God (the scriptures), pray with thanksgiving and supplications, and seek to share this glorious hope with others so that our lives increasingly reflect the obedience of Christ.

Anchored in Christ

What Is In A Name?

In this lesson we have been learning about naming our variables according to the rules in Python. If a variable is named incorrectly, then Python has no idea what to do with it. The entire program is wrong, just because one little name is out of place. When everything is named according to the rules, but time has not been taking to name the variables clearly and carefully, then confusion can arise as to what the code is even doing, even though it technically works. Having a name, having a clear name, and a name with purpose is important. Yes, its even important in life.

In the scriptures, your name is everything. It defines you. For a very large part of human history it was understood that when you were part of a family, you bore the family name. You could honor that family name, or dishonor it. The preservation of the family name was everything. That might seem a little odd to us now here in the 21st century with our increases sense of individuality, but God never intended us to live as individual lone rangers. The sense of belonging to a community and being named by the community is still very important for His image bearers.

In the book of Ephesians we read Paul tell the church: “So then you are no longer strangers and aliens, but you are fellow citizens with the saints and members of the household of God.” Did you know that another wonderful blessing in the heavenly places is that Christ won back a name for you in His Father's house? If you trust in Christ, you are His now. You are named by Him, and that name is eternal.
