Challenge: Create Your Own Text-Based Adventure

Build a short interactive story using Python

You have learned conditionals (if / elif / else), comparison operators, lists, and loops. Now it is time to put those skills together and create your own short text-based adventure game.

Your Mission

Write a Python program that tells a short adventure story. The player should be able to make choices that change what happens next. The story can be set in a forest, a castle, a spaceship, or any wholesome setting you enjoy.

Requirements

  • Use at least three different decision points (if / elif / else)
  • Store the player’s inventory or important items in a list
  • Allow the player to play through the story more than once (a loop)
  • Give clear feedback after every choice
  • Include a way for the player to quit the game

Stretch Goals (optional)

  • Keep score or track how many good choices the player made
  • Use negative indexing or len() when working with the inventory list
  • Add a “secret” path that only appears if the player has a certain item

Start small. A story with three or four scenes is perfect.
Focus on clear choices and fun outcomes.

When you finish, share your adventure with a classmate or your teacher.
