Lesson 007 - int(), float() and str() Functions — Talking Points

Key talking points and discussion notes for teaching converting between types with int(), float(), and str(), especially with user-facing values.

Main Teaching Points

  • Three converters: int, float, str—what each does.

  • Strings from outside: input and text need conversion before math.

  • Errors: TypeError vs ValueError—what students will see.

  • Step conversions: float then int for "42.5".

  • Dependence on Christ: We do not transform ourselves (Eph 2)—teach with reverence.

Discussion & Talking Points

  • Question 1: Why does "5" + "5" differ from int("5") + int("5")?

  • Question 2: What happens with int(3.9)? Is that rounding?

  • Question 3: How would you convert a price typed by a user into a float?

  • Question 4: Why might int("3.14") fail?

  • Question 5: How does conversion we cannot do for ourselves point to our need for Christ?

Biblical Integration

A string like "37" cannot turn itself into the integer 37. In a deeper way, we cannot raise ourselves from spiritual death to life. We depend on Christ’s transforming work and faith in Him.

Scripture (NKJV):
“And you He made alive, who were dead in trespasses and sins… even when we were dead in trespasses, made us alive together with Christ (by grace you have been saved).” (Ephesians 2:1, 5, NKJV)
