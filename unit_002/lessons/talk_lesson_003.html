Lesson 003 - Data Types and Type Conversion — Talking Points

Key talking points and discussion notes for teaching int, float, str, bool, the type() function, and converting between basic types.

Main Teaching Points

  • Four basics: int, float, str, bool—what each stores and common examples.

  • type() as a detective: Use it when students are confused about a value.

  • Operations depend on type: + means add for numbers, concatenate for strings.

  • Conversion is deliberate: int/float/str when input or output needs a different kind of value.

  • Truth with grace: Different people needed different approaches; Christ always applied truth wisely—use carefully as analogy.

Discussion & Talking Points

  • Question 1: What is the difference between 7 and "7"?

  • Question 2: Why does "2" + "3" produce "23"?

  • Question 3: When would you convert a string to an int in a real program?

  • Question 4: What does type(True) report?

  • Question 5: How can “right tool for the value” remind us that Jesus always applied truth wisely and compassionately?

Biblical Integration

Different data need different handling. Jesus always knew what truth each person needed, and He gave truth with mercy and grace—light that exposes darkness and leads to Himself.

Scripture (NKJV):
“Then Jesus spoke to them again, saying, ‘I am the light of the world. He who follows Me shall not walk in darkness, but have the light of life.’” (John 8:12, NKJV)
