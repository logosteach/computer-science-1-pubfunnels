Lesson 004 - Printing and Escape Characters — Talking Points

Key talking points and discussion notes for teaching print() with multiple arguments, separators, and escape sequences like \n and \t.

Main Teaching Points

  • print args, sep, end: Multiple values, custom separators, controlling newlines.

  • Escape sequences: \n, \t, \\, \" — show the raw string vs. displayed result.

  • Layout for humans: Tables with tabs, headers, separators—output is communication.

  • Clarity of the gospel: Connect carefully: clear output vs. God making Christ known as light.

Discussion & Talking Points

  • Question 1: What does sep="-" change in print("A", "B", "C", sep="-")?

  • Question 2: How do you print a backslash character itself?

  • Question 3: Why might tabs help format a simple table in the console?

  • Question 4: What is the difference between two print calls and print(..., end=" ")?

  • Question 5: How does striving for clear output parallel the clarity of the gospel message?

Biblical Integration

We use escapes so output is clear and readable. God has made the gospel clear as light in the darkness—calling us to look to Christ alone.

“For it is the God who commanded light to shine out of darkness, who has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.” (2 Corinthians 4:6, NKJV)

“Then Jesus spoke to them again, saying, ‘I am the light of the world…’” (John 8:12, NKJV)
