Python Variables and Naming Rules

In this lesson, we will explore the concept of variables in Python. Variables are fundamental building blocks in programming that allow us to store and manipulate data. We will learn how to declare variables, assign values to them, and use them in your code. We will discuss the ideas of referencing and de-referencing and the consequences that happen in computer memory when a value becomes de-referenced. We will also attempt to contrast the mutable nature of variable assignment with the immutable promises of our heavenly Father, and of Christ. (Hebrews 13:8).

Objectives

By the end of the lesson, learners will be able to:

  • Declare and assign values to variables in Python.

  • Understand variable naming rules and best practices.

  • Re-assign values to variables and explain the concept of mutability.

  • Use variables in mathematical expressions and output.

  • Contrast the mutable nature of variables in Python (which can change value) with the immutable promises of God, which are "Yes and Amen!" in Christ Jesus and never change, giving us a firm foundation we can always rely on (2 Corinthians 1:20).

The Stand

Their class is studying revenue — the money that comes in when something is sold. Their teacher will sit with them first and settle what each cookie and each brownie should be priced at for this sale. You will not invent those two prices, unless you are working on this lab individually without teacher assistance. You will store the prices your teacher gives you, store how many of each treat were sold, and print the revenue.
