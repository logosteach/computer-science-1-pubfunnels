# Lesson 2 - While Loops

Have you ever had anyone tell you, "Just wait a while.."? If you are like me, you really didn't want to. When we are forced to stop and wait for something, this kind of models a while loop. Some type of condition needs to be met until the waiting is over. Just imagine the promise of the redeemer given by God in Genesis 3:15. And then.. what happened? We had to wait. Generation after generation, we had to wait until the promised Messiah would come to smash the head of the serpent and restore what our first father Adam had lost.

We no longer wait for the first coming of the Messiah do we? We now wait and anticipate his second coming. Yet just like the timing of Christ's first coming had to be at just the right time, so too will his second coming be. At just the right time.

"But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth His Son, made of a woman, made under the law, To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons. And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying Abba, Father. Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ." - Galatians 4:4-7 KJV
