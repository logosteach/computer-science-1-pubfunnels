Introduction to Loops

In this lesson, we will explore the fundamental programming concept of loops. Loops allow us to execute a block of code repeatedly, which is essential for performing tasks that require repetition. We will cover the two primary types of loops in Python: the while loop and the for loop.

The Lord Himself has established loops in His creation. From the cycles of day to night, the changing of the tides and seasons, God has promised that while the earth endures these things shall not cease. Yes, we are responsible to take care of this creation our compassionate and merciful God has given to us, yet, we are also warned and comforted that nothing we can do will bring the earth to its end. The life and death of this creation, lies in the hands of our mighty God. He will keep his promises. (Genesis 8:22; Psalm 104:19)

Objectives

By the end of the lesson, learners will be able to:

  • Explain why repetition is necessary in programming and give real-world examples of repetitive tasks.

  • Describe the two main types of loops in Python: while and for.

  • Understand the basic concept of iteration and how loops execute code repeatedly.

  • Recognize the danger of infinite loops and how to prevent them.

  • Reflect on how loops illustrate the importance of rhythm and order in God’s creation, just as seasons, day and night, and the cycles of life show His faithful and consistent character (Genesis 8:22; Psalm 104:19).
