The While Loop

In this lesson, we will explore the concept of loops in programming, focusing on the while loop. While loops are best used when you know you want a process to repeat and indefinite number of times. You then set a condition or a type of "flag" that lets the program know when to stop the loop. While loops are powerful tools for creating interactive programs such as: video games, user input forms, simulations, and much more.

The Lord Himself has His perfect timing in our lives. Perhaps the most relevant while loop to the Christian believer is the parable of the persistent widow in Luke 18:1-8. Do not give up in prayer! Never give up, never surrender. The Lord will answer in His own perfect timing. Our job is to be faithful and trust. This teaches us more of His good and kind character in and through the face of Jesus Christ our Lord and Savior. (Philippians 4:6-7).

Objectives

By the end of the lesson, learners will be able to:

  • Write and understand the syntax of a while loop.

  • Use a while loop with a counter or condition that eventually becomes false.

  • Combine while loops with user input to create interactive programs.

  • Identify and avoid infinite loops by ensuring the loop condition eventually changes.

  • Reflect on how the while loop reminds us that persistence in prayer and faithfulness is important, yet we must also know when to stop and trust God’s timing (Luke 18:1-8; Ecclesiastes 3:1).
