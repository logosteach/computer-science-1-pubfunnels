# Lesson 4 - Loop Control Statements: Break and Continue

Have you even been trapped before? I mean really trapped in something like a large trunk, or perhaps a room you couldn't get out of? It can be terrifying. I hope that something like that has never happened to you before, yet, we have to acknowledge that that is exactly where we use to be before we saw the light of the gospel. We were trapped.

In this lesson, we are learning how to break free from loops prematurely as well as learning how to skip certain iterations of a loop when they are encountered. The first command we learn is the break command, the second is the continue command.

And yet, the whole of the Christian life is break, continue, break, continue.. Let me try to explain what I mean.

When you saw the light of the gospel and trusted in Christ alone He set you free from the power of sin, but not its presence. Therefore, we are no longer obligated to obey sin, but we still struggle with it and we can still fall into it. Because of the work of God in you, we are called by His Spirit to break free from our former mindset of the flesh, and then continue in His grace, growing into Christ more and more. And so, the entire Christian life is, break out of the former patterns of my fleshly life, and continue in His grace. By God's grace, as we grow in the vine that is Christ, we end up continuing more than breaking.

(Romans 8:12-14, KJV)
12 Therefore, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live after the flesh.
13 For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die: but if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live.
14 For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.

Be encouraged today. You have a strong savior who intercedes for you before His father and your father in heaven.
